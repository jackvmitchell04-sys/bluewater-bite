Marks

Solid yellow: official or surveyed positions. Hollow orange (≈): approximate, worked out from skipper descriptions and depth contours. Pink: your own marks, saved on this device.

Catch log

Saves where and when you hooked up, with the water temp, front distance, depth, moon and tide at that spot. Uses your GPS if ◎ is on, otherwise the last point you tapped. Stored on this device.

Data inputs

Waypoints and trolling courses

Data: NASA JPL MUR SST and climatology, NOAA ACSPO Himawari-9 hourly SST, NOAA VIIRS + Copernicus Sentinel-3 OLCI chlorophyll and Kd490, NOAA altimetry (SSH), NIWA Bay of Plenty 25 m bathymetry, GEBCO 2020, DOC and BOPRC protected areas via NOAA CoastWatch ERDDAP; ECMWF IFS wind, Copernicus currents, SST forecast and tides via Open-Meteo. Not for navigation: use official charts. Moon and sun positions computed on device. Scores are a planning aid, not a guarantee. Always check the MetService marine forecast and Coastguard NZ before running offshore.
